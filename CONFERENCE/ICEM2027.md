## Programme (as published)

- Three days of plenary presentations, parallel technical sessions, poster sessions, industry-led workshops and networking events
- Follows the ICEM 2025 format, with new industry-led workshops and more focus on AI for weather, climate and energy
- The programme page gives no timetable yet; it "will be updated regularly as keynote speakers, workshops and technical sessions are confirmed"
- ICEM 2025 brochure: https://www.wemcouncil.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/icem2025_brochure_20250601_final.pdf

## Abstract submission rules

- Length: at most 3,000 characters including spaces
- One simple table or figure allowed (PNG or JPG, uploaded separately)
- Structured abstract preferred, not mandatory: Objective & Background, Method, Results, Conclusion
- Substantive results preferred; references used sparingly; abbreviations spelled out at first use
- Fee: €30 incl. IVA, refunded only if the abstract is unsuccessful
- Steps: create a customer account, pay the fee, follow the link in the confirmation email to the platform (MZ Events): https://ems.mzevents.it/start/2638/eng
- Preferred presentation format is stated at submission; the Scientific Committee decides
- An accepted abstract enters the programme only if the presenting author registers for at least one day
- Posters: A0 portrait, one-minute oral presentation, displayed for the whole conference
- Awards: Best Student Presentation (oral or poster); Best Poster
